## PdfFormatConversionOptions.AutoTaggingSettings property

Gets or sets the settings for automatic tagging during PDF format conversion.

Automatic tagging settings are used to configure the behavior of the auto-tagging process, 
 which is typically employed to enhance the accessibility and structure of a PDF document 
 during conversion to a specific PDF format.

public AutoTaggingSettings AutoTaggingSettings { get; set; }
