Contains classes and interfaces for recognizing and analyzing the structural composition of PDF documents. This includes components such as [`StructureRecognitionVisitor`](aspose.pdf.flow/structurerecognitionvisitor/), which traverses the layout tree to identify elements like paragraphs, tables, sections, and figures, and to build a structured representation of the document.
